Privacy

Nothing comes to us

There’s no Bones Companion server. The app on your computer talks straight to the AI company you pick, and to GitHub to check for updates.

  • There’s no Bones Companion account

    Nothing to sign up for with us. The only account you might need is at your AI company.

  • Your key stays on your computer

    It’s kept in your macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager and sent only to your AI company. It never goes into the game.

  • Nothing tracks you

    No analytics, telemetry or crash reports, in the app or on this page.

  • It never plays for you

    No key presses or mouse clicks, no memory reading, nothing posted to chat. The AI can only write text, which the addon turns into replies, routes and gear notes.

  • You see every connection

    The app lists every connection it makes and shows exactly what it sent last.

  • The app reads one corner of the game

    The app reads only the small corner its addon draws in the game, so your messages go at once. On a Mac, that needs Screen Recording. You can use Bones without screen reading.

By default, your character’s name, realm and guild stay out of what’s sent, and other players’ names are swapped for stand-ins.

Questions

Common questions

Can I use my Claude or ChatGPT subscription?

No. A Claude, ChatGPT or Grok subscription isn’t an API key and doesn’t include API credit. You need an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI with a little pay-as-you-go credit ($5 lasts about 2–4 weeks), or you can try Bones free with OpenRouter’s free models.

What does the app send to my AI?

Your message, your character’s game data (level, class, race, zone and position, money, experience, talents, professions, quests and gear) and the app’s short notes on your progress. You can leave the game data out of any message. Your character’s name, realm and guild stay out unless you turn them on, other players’ names are swapped for stand-ins, and other players’ chat is never read.

Is my API key safe?

Your key is kept in your macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager and sent only to the AI company it belongs to. It never goes into the game, to us or into logs.

Also set a spend limit at your AI company. If a key ever leaks, that limit is what stops the bill.

Does Bones play for me?

No. Bones Companion never presses keys, moves the mouse, reads the game’s memory or posts to chat. The AI can only write text, which the addon turns into replies, routes and gear notes. Bones answers your messages, and speaks first only for check-ins, which you turn on.

Can I use Bones without screen reading?

Yes. Turn off Screen Reading in the addon’s Settings (under What Bones Knows): nothing is drawn; your messages wait for a reload, and replies still come in. To send a waiting message, click Reload above the window. The game shows a short loading screen, and it can’t reload during a fight.

Is Bones Companion allowed?

Bones Companion is unofficial: Blizzard hasn’t made, reviewed or endorsed it. It reads the small corner its addon draws in the game and writes addon files, and it never controls your character. Blizzard’s rules are Blizzard’s to apply, so use it at your own risk.

How do I check my download?

Every release lists a SHA-256 checksum for each file in SHA256SUMS.txtSHA256SUMS.txt. On a Mac, open Terminal, type shasum -a 256 and a space, drag the file into the window and press Enter. On Windows, open PowerShell, type Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 and a space, drag the file into the window and press Enter. The checksum it prints should match the file’s line in SHA256SUMS.txt (Windows prints it in capital letters; that still matches).

On Windows, right-click the installer, click Properties, then the Digital Signatures tab to see who signed it. On a Mac, macOS confirms Apple has checked the app the first time you open it.

Which version of WoW does it work with?

World of Warcraft: Forever, in Windowed or Windowed (Fullscreen) mode. Other versions of WoW aren’t supported.
